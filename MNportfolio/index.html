About me

A student builder, still in progress.

I'm Marie Anjilee V. Naces, a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology student at Cebu Eastern College. I'm using my classes to practice designing and building real, usable software, from the first screen sketch to a working page.

My projects tend to centre on helping people and animals: booking a pet clinic visit, reporting a stray, or adopting a rescued pet. I also enjoy making something playful, like a browser version of Patintero, a classic Filipino street game.

Most of my projects are still being built, and I label them honestly below. I'm here to keep learning.
